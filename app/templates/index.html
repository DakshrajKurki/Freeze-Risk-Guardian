How this would actually integrate

This is a proposal, not a built integration: score the full merchant base as a nightly batch job (reusing the exact /api/batch-assess endpoint already in this app), surface flagged accounts to the compliance team's existing review queue, and re-run the What-If Simulator's logic at KYC-document-update time so a merchant's risk score updates the moment they fix something — closing the loop between "flagged" and "resolved" instead of leaving merchants guessing.
